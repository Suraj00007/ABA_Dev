How it was built

Everything is Salesforce metadata kept in this SFDX project and deployed with the Salesforce CLI. The reports, dashboards and Home page are generated by a script, so a change is made once in code and redeployed. Nobody has to rebuild 20 reports by hand.

Steps, in order

  1. Retrieved the org into the project.Full metadata pulled with a manifest generated from the org (manifest/full-package.xml), so the starting point was under source control.
  2. Configured the data model per sheet 4 of the build guide: new stages, Opportunity Type and Lead Source values, five new opportunity fields and one account field, a validation rule, a flow and a permission set. Deployed and checked.
  3. Cleared the default demo data.The org's 31 out-of-the-box sample opportunities (Edge, GenePoint, etc.) were exported to data/backup/ and deleted, so they don't inflate ABA's numbers.
  4. Created two salesperson users and loaded the sample accounts and opportunities with scripts/aba/build_sample_data.py.
  5. Generated and deployed the reports with scripts/aba/build_reports.py. Every report was then run through the Salesforce Analytics API, and its record count and total matched an independent SOQL query (20 of 20).
  6. Generated the two dashboards and the Home page, assigned the page to the Sales app, and checked them in the browser.

Report catalog

The 20 reports

All reports use the standard Opportunities report type and All opportunities scope. Management reports are in ABA Management Reports, shared with the CEO role and below. The rest are in ABA Sales Reports, shared with all internal users. CORE marks the 10 reports committed in the proposal. Click a report to see its full specification.

Results are from 8 Oct 2026. Every relative date filter (this month, this fiscal year, next 30 days) moves with the calendar, so the numbers change as days pass.

Lightning dashboards

Dashboards

Both dashboards are in the folder ABA Management Dashboards (shared with the CEO role and below) on a 12-column grid. They run as Suraj OD, so every viewer sees the full picture, and both have Opportunity Owner and Account Industry filters. The layout below matches the real component positions.

First open after a deploy shows “No data”. Salesforce only builds dashboard data when the dashboard is refreshed. Before the demo, click Refresh on each dashboard. Each one can be refreshed at most once a minute.

Suggested script · about 15 minutes

Demo walkthrough

  1. Open Sales → Home.“This is what management sees when they log in.” Point to the top row of D1: 2.75M won this month, 2.75M this quarter against a 5M target (55%, so the gauge is red), 8.9M year to date, 31.4M active pipeline and a 60% win rate.
  2. Growth trend (R04).Compare FY2025 and FY2026 quarter by quarter. Click the chart to drill into the report.
  3. Pipeline (R05, R06).The funnel is by stage. Deferred and Old / Archived deals are excluded, as agreed on Day 3. Then show which quarters the pipeline is expected to close in, split by forecast category.
  4. Deals to push now (R11).Three deals at Proposal Submitted or later close in the next 30 days, worth 4.7M. This uses the same rule as the planned 30-day reminder e-mail.
  5. Use the dashboard filters.Set Opportunity Owner to one salesperson, then Account Industry to Banking. Every component updates together.
  6. Sales performance (D2).Leaderboard: Venkatesh 4.2M, Alburayh 3.3M, Fouad 1.4M. Win rates: Alburayh 80%, Fouad 67%, Venkatesh 43%.
  7. Why we win and lose (R10).Price competitiveness drives most wins (5.6M). Losses are budget cuts (3.1M) and competitors (2.7M). This is why Win/Loss Reason is a picklist and not free text.
  8. Hygiene and deferred deals (R12, R13).Two overdue deals need follow-up. 11.5M is on hold in Deferred and can be reopened.
  9. Show an opportunity record.Open the Bid & Win-Loss section, then try to close a deal without a reason to show the validation rule.
  10. Close by asking for confirmations.Stages, win/loss reasons, the quarterly target, currency and fiscal year. Walk through the Open items list.
If ABA asks for changes

Making changes

There are two ways to change things. Small tweaks during or after the demo can be made directly in Salesforce Setup or the report builder. Lasting changes should go into the scripts and be redeployed, so the project and the org stay in sync.

python scripts/aba/build_reports.py
sf project deploy start -x manifest/aba-package.xml -o ABA_Dev

The first command regenerates the reports, dashboards, Home page and the manifest. The second deploys all 44 ABA components. Add --dry-run to validate without deploying. If you edit a report in the Salesforce UI, pull it back into the project afterwards with sf project retrieve start -m "Report:ABA_Sales_Reports/R08_Revenue_Won_by_Salesperson_YTD".
